# 🚆 TrackGenie — Intelligent Railway Maintenance Optimization

### Plan Smarter. Respond Faster. Keep Trains Moving.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

- 🛠️ Railway tracks and assets require regular maintenance.
- 🚆 Maintenance blocks must be planned around running trains.
- ⚠️ Poor scheduling can create train conflicts and delays.
- 🔄 Train delays and unexpected events can make existing plans invalid.
- 🚨 Emergency maintenance requires quick decision-making.

---

## 📌 Key Facts

- Railway maintenance requires temporary track/asset blocks.
- Train schedules and maintenance activities must be carefully coordinated.
- A change in train operations can affect an existing maintenance plan.
- Emergency failures require immediate maintenance planning.
- Manual decision-making can become difficult when multiple constraints change.

---

## 💡 Introducing TrackGenie

**TrackGenie** is an **Agentic AI-powered decision-support platform** that automatically analyzes railway conditions and recommends optimized maintenance blocks with minimum operational disruption.

---

## 📋 Problem Statement

> To develop an intelligent system that automatically plans and dynamically replans railway maintenance blocks by considering train schedules, track availability, maintenance requirements, priorities and operational conflicts.

---

## 🎯 Our Goals

- 🤖 Automate maintenance block planning.
- ⚠️ Minimize train conflicts and delays.
- 🔄 Dynamically replan when conditions change.
- 🚨 Support emergency maintenance decisions.
- 📊 Help officers compare and choose better plans.
- 👨‍💼 Keep human approval in the decision-making loop.

---

## 👥 Target Audience

- 🚦 Railway Operations / Control Officers
- 🛠️ Maintenance & Engineering Officers
- 📋 Railway Planning Staff

---

## ⚙️ Core Features

- 🤖 **AI Maintenance Block Planning**
- ⚠️ **Train Conflict Detection**
- 🔄 **Dynamic Re-planning**
- 🔮 **What-If Simulation**
- 🚨 **Emergency Maintenance Mode**
- 🗺️ **Interactive Railway Network**
- 📊 **Delay & Impact Prediction**
- 🔍 **Explainable AI Recommendations**

---

## 🌟 What Makes TrackGenie Different?

- 🤖 Agentic AI instead of only static scheduling.
- 🔄 Automatically adapts to changing railway conditions.
- 🔮 Allows planners to test scenarios before approval.
- 🚨 Handles both planned and emergency maintenance.
- 🔍 Explains the reasoning behind recommendations.
- 👨‍💼 AI recommends; the authorized officer makes the final decision.

---

## 📊 Feature Comparison

| Capability | Traditional Planning | TrackGenie |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance Planning | Manual / Schedule-based | 🤖 AI-assisted |
| Conflict Detection | Manual checking | ⚠️ Automatic |
| Schedule Changes | Manual re-planning | 🔄 Dynamic re-planning |
| What-If Analysis | Limited | 🔮 Built-in |
| Delay Prediction | Manual assessment | 📊 AI-based |
| Emergency Planning | Manual coordination | 🚨 AI-assisted |
| Explainable Recommendation | Limited | 🔍 Yes |
